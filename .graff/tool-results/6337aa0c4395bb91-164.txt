  120 | /// project checkout may be indexed on the fly without CODEDB_ALLOW_TEMP.
  121 | /// Rationale: agent harnesses hard-fail once on a refused call and never
  122 | /// retry the tool for the rest of the session, so refusing a legit /tmp
  123 | /// clone silently disables codedb entirely. System prefixes (/usr, /etc, …)
